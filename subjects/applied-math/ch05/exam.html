$$\begin{aligned}&(2l+1)\int_0^1P_l\,dx\\&=\Big[P_{l+1}-P_{l-1}\Big]_0^1\\&=(1-1)-\big(P_{l+1}(0)-P_{l-1}(0)\big)\end{aligned}$$ $\displaystyle\int_0^1P_l\,dx=\frac{P_{l-1}(0)-P_{l+1}(0)}{2l+1}$
